PureTV

Connect to your TV

PureTV talks to Android TV and Google TV over your Wi-Fi. Nothing is uninstalled, and everything can be undone.

  1. On the TV open Settings → System → About and press OK on Android TV OS build seven times.
  2. Go back to System → Developer options and turn on USB debugging (and Network debugging, if shown).
  3. Find the TV's IP address in Settings → Network & Internet → your Wi-Fi, and type it below.

The TV will ask “Allow USB debugging?” — tick Always allow and press Allow.

Back up first

Before anything changes, PureTV writes down every app's on/off state, your Home screen and the TV's settings. Restore replays it exactly.

Clean

Chosen from community-tested TV lists. Apps are switched off, never deleted. Core system parts are locked.

Uses Android's Private DNS with AdGuard DNS: ad and tracker domains stop loading in every app. It does not block YouTube ads, and AdGuard sees which sites the TV looks up. Leave it off if your router already filters (Pi-hole, AdGuard Home, a VPN). Restore turns it off again.

A calmer Home screen

PureTV Home replaces the ad-filled stock home with a clock, your apps and nothing else. No ads, no recommendations, no network access.

One last thing on the TV

Turn USB debugging, Network debugging and Wireless debugging off in Developer options, then reboot the TV and press Check debugging is off. PureTV Home keeps working without it; you only need it back to clean again or restore.

After a firmware update

An update can switch some apps back on and give Home back to the stock launcher. This finds them and switches them off again.
