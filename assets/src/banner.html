For Android TV & Google TV

PureTV

Clean your TV, remove the ads, and give it a beautiful tvOS‑inspired home.

✓Switches off ads, tracking and preloaded junk
✓Backs up your TV first. Nothing is deleted
✓One press puts everything back

What you need

An Android TV or Google TVTCL, Sony, Xiaomi, Hisense, Acer, Philips, Chromecast with Google TV…
A computer on the same Wi‑FiWindows, macOS or Linux. Only needed while setting up
Python 3Free from python.org. PureTV sets up the rest by itself

Install in four steps about ten minutes

1
Unlock Developer options
Settings›System›About
Press OK on Android TV OS build seven times until it says you are a developer.
2
Turn on USB debugging
System›Developer options›USB debugging
Also turn on Network debugging if your TV shows it.

Only Wireless debugging? PureTV pairs with its code too.

3
Download and start
Releases›PureTV‑1.1.zip
Unzip, then open Start PureTV.bat on Windows or start.sh on Mac and Linux.
4
Find, back up, clean
Find my TV›Back up›Clean›Launcher
Press Allow on the TV when it asks. When done, turn debugging off again.
